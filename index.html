Interactive Itinerary

DAY 1 · SEP 23 – ARRIVAL DAY
  • 08:15 AM – Group A lands in Guwahati. Board buses, breakfast on the road.
  • Group B lands in Guwahati. Convoy with Group A.
  • Umiam Lake – Boating & Views. En-route stop, 45 mins. Misty hills, tea stalls.
  • Group C arrives Shillong. Early check-in at Alpine Continental.
  • All 97 check in – Alpine Continental. Freshen up, rest, regroup.
  • Police Bazar Market Walk – shopping & local snacks.
  • Welcome Dinner – Meghalayan cuisine (Jadoh, Doh Khlieh & more).
DAY 2 · SEP 24 – CHERRAPUNJI DAY
  • 08:00 AM – After breakfast, depart for Cherrapunji.
  • Elephant Falls – Easy walk, 3-tiered beauty.
  • Shillong Peak – Drive up, panoramic views.
  • Mawkdok – Bungee Jump (200 ft) (Pre-registered members only).
  • Nohkalikai Falls – Tallest plunge waterfall in India.
  • Seven Sisters Falls – Iconic multi-tiered view.
  • Mawsmai Cave – Easy 150m cave walk.
  • Return to Shillong.
  • Dinner & Relax.
DAY 3 · SEP 25 – DAWKI CAMPING NIGHT
  • 07:00 AM – Early breakfast & partial checkout.
  • Arrive Dawki – Umngot River.
  • Boat rides – crystal clear water!
  • Riverside lunch & free time.
  • Corporate team games & activities.
  • Bonfire BBQ dinner – DJ & music.
  • Camping under the stars by the river / Bangladesh Border Scenic Drive – panoramic drive on the river bank.
DAY 4 · SEP 26 – MAWLYNNONG & BACK TO SHILLONG
  • 06:00 AM – Sunrise at Dawki (Optional early morning boat ride).
  • Breakfast & depart.
  • Mawlynnong – Asia's Cleanest Village. Sky Walk – Flat & scenic. Bamboo dustbins, flower-lined paths.
  • Riwai Living Root Bridge – easy trek to a natural marvel.
  • Check-in – Alpine Continental, Shillong.
  • Grand Farewell Dinner. Speeches, awards, memories!
DAY 5 · SEP 27 – HOMEWARD BOUND
  • 07:06 AM – Breakfast & Checkout.
  • Wards Lake – peaceful walk & optional boating.
  • Depart Shillong. 3.5 hr drive to Guwahati Airport via NH6.
  • Guwahati Airport. Lunch, check-in, security, lounge.
  • Flights Depart – All groups fly home. Safe travels, team!
